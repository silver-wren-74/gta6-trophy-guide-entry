Roadmap to the Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

Built from the official descriptions and counts. One playthrough is enough: no trophy needs a second run if you protect the single missable step in Stage 1. Estimated total is dominated by the 100% completion Gold, A Long Road.

  1. 1

    Story first, missable protected

    Play the campaign in order: Gone Fishing, Chapters 1–4, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown and Operation Savage Glory, through to the final mission (Gold: You Gotta Know When to Get Off). During Chapter 1, meditate at the spot at Jason's home — it belongs to Positive Mental Attitude, the list's only missable trophy. Turn on the spoiler shield below if you want story names hidden until you earn them.

  2. 2

    Strangers & strands

    Finish full stranger strands: Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each carry their own Bronze, Bret's unique-animal hunt gives Fair Game, and any 8 finished strands (of 14) earn the Silver Strangers & Freaks. Finish strands completely — started strands count for nothing.

  3. 3

    Activities & skill

    Sample all 9 activity types (Master of None), then win: Top Fun drone races at all 14 locations, 20 different races, Cal's fishing challenge, one arcade game, and a full game at the Kolt & Associates bowling alley. Grind the Silver The Shootist — gold medals in all 42 Shooting Range challenges — across several sessions, and max out strength for both Jason and Lucia (Personal Best).

  4. 4

    Crime wave

    Rob 5 bank vaults and escape (Silver: Maximum Withdrawal), an armored truck, 3 shops back-to-back with your partner, and a jewelry store unseen. Rob one Illegal Operation for each of the 9 gangs, steal a Rhino tank, make an unscheduled departure from VCIA, drop 5 enemies in one Combat Focus, lose a 4-star Wanted level with your partner, reach 500 Criminal Profile actions, and launder $250,000 in Marked Bills.

  5. 5

    Collectibles & the map

    Complete 3 Collectible Card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, meditate at all 18 spots, complete 250 stunts, explore all 52 areas of Leonida, discover 100 vehicles and register 10 of them in the Scooter Bros app, spend $100,000 in shops, buy all 8 garage properties, and finish 50 of the 80 challenges.

  6. 6

    100% — A Long Road

    Close out the in-game completion checklist to 100% for the second Gold. Whatever counters remain — stunts, areas, vehicles, challenges — are fastest finished category by category from the game's own percentage screen. When the last of the 54 pops, Vice Got a Price! follows automatically.

Missable watch: only Positive Mental Attitude is flagged missable — one of its 18 meditation spots sits at Jason's home in Chapter 1. Every other trophy on this list can still be earned after the story ends. Five trophies (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) have unconfirmed requirements in the guides consulted on 20 Nov 2026; their cards say what is reported and invent nothing.
